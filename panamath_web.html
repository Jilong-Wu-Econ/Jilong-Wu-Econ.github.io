Dot Comparison Task

Approximate Number System (ANS) Test

On each trial you will see a brief flash of yellow and blue dots displayed in two separate windows side by side.

Your task is to judge which color had more dots. The dots flash quickly — go with your first impression and do not try to count.

After the dots disappear, click the Yellow or Blue button to record your answer. You can also press the Y or B key on your keyboard.

There will be 4 practice trials to get familiar, then 40 test trials. Your ANS acuity (Weber fraction) will be calculated at the end.

Practice Complete

You are now ready for the real task

The task is identical to what you just practiced. Remember: judge which color has more dots and respond quickly — do not try to count.

There will be 40 trials in total.
